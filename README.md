## Overview of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme’s project management approach is designed for clarity, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Projects begin with dedicated initiation: a one-pager aligns business need, stakeholders, and success metrics right from the start. Planning breaks initiatives into actionable deliverables, prioritizes backlogs, estimates effort, and identifies risks and communication strategies—all using standardized templates and checklists.

Distinct roles are defined to drive accountability and effective collaboration. The Project Manager (PM) coordinates scheduling, risk, and communication; the Product Manager (PdM) shapes problems and measures outcomes; developers build and test features, and QA ensures that every deliverable meets OctoAcme’s high quality bar. These responsibilities are supported by daily standups, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, and transparent project boards for maximum visibility.

Communication is foundational: daily check-ins provide tactical alignment, while weekly delivery and demo reviews keep broader goals and milestones on track. Teams document project status, risk, and decisions, sharing through regular reports and dashboards. A clear escalation path allows for prompt resolution of critical issues, minimizing disruption while maximizing stakeholder awareness.

Quality assurance practices are embedded at every stage. Unit, integration, and end-to-end tests are required for all new work; smoke tests and manual QA verify that releases meet all criteria before deployment. Retrospectives and continuous improvement reviews follow each milestone or release, capturing learnings, driving action, and closing the loop between process and product quality.
